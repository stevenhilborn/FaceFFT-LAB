3 · Your mathematical formula

For each color channel c ∈ {R,G,B}, the exact discrete reconstruction equation is:

f_c(x,y) = (1 / (W × H)) × Σ[u=0..W−1] Σ[v=0..H−1]
           (A_c[u,v] + i B_c[u,v]) × exp(2πi(ux/W + vy/H))

Pixel_c(x,y) = clamp(round(Re(f_c(x,y))), 0, 255)

The downloadable plain-text formula contains A_c and B_c for every frequency.
